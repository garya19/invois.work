How it works

From setup to first invoice
in under 5 minutes

01

Find Invois on Telegram

Invois lives entirely inside Telegram — the messaging app you already use every day. No new app to download, no website to log into, no dashboard to learn. Just open @sendinvois_bot and you’re ready. Everything happens through simple menu buttons.

02

Set up your profile

Enter your business name, email, and upload your logo. Takes 2 minutes — once done, your branding appears on every invoice automatically.

03

Add your clients

Save each client’s name, mobile, email, and city. Takes 30 seconds per client. Edit or update anytime from the bot menu.

04

Create and send invoices

Pick a client, add your line items, choose a tax, and preview the PDF. One tap sends it to their email with the PDF attached.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don’t.

Built for solo coaches and tutors, not accountants. Every feature is reachable with 2–3 taps.

Complete privacy — yours and your clients’

Your invoice data and your clients’ details belong to you alone. We never sell, share, or access your data. Subscription payments are processed by industry-standard payment processors — we never see your card or bank details.

Professional PDF invoices

Your logo, business name, client details, line items, and totals — formatted as a clean, professional PDF every time.

Email + Telegram delivery

Send invoices to your client’s email with the PDF attached, or share directly in Telegram if they’re on it.

Tax invoice support

GST, VAT, HST, or custom tax — choose per invoice. The PDF header automatically shows “Tax Invoice” when applicable.

Payment reminders

Send a gentle nudge to clients who haven’t paid. Re-attach the PDF or send a text-only reminder — your choice.

Track who has paid

Mark invoices paid with one tap. See all unpaid invoices at a glance — no spreadsheet, no sticky notes.

CSV & Google Sheets export

Export your customers and invoices to a CSV that opens in Excel, or push directly to your own Google Sheet.

Client address book

Store client name, mobile, email, and city. Edit anytime. Reuse in seconds — no retyping addresses for returning clients.

Multi-currency

INR for India, USD, EUR, GBP, or any currency for international clients. The PDF shows the correct symbol automatically.

  • Itemised line items with quantity × rate
  • Tax breakdown (GST, VAT, HST, or custom)

  • Sent as email attachment — clients can forward it to their accounts team
