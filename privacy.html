Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 5 October 2026

Short version: We store only what you give us. We never sell your data. We never share it with third parties except our essential payment and email service providers. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

By accessing and using the bot, you explicitly acknowledge and agree to this Privacy Policy and consent to the data practices described below.

1. Who we are

@sendinvois_bot is operated by Citadel, a messaging platform-based invoicing tool for independent coaches, tutors, and freelancers. You can contact us at hi@invois.work.

2. What data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the invoicing service:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and messaging platform user ID when you start the bot.
  • Business information: Your business name, address, logo, and payment identifiers used to populate your invoices.
  • Client records: Names, mobile numbers, email addresses, and cities of clients you add to the bot. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to store this information.
  • Invoice data: Line items, amounts, tax settings, invoice dates, and payment status for invoices you create.
  • Payment receipts: Transaction IDs you provide when subscribing. We do not store card numbers, bank account details, or PINs.
  • Usage data: Basic anonymised analytics. This data is processed securely and does not identify you personally.

3. How we use your data

  • To generate and deliver PDF invoices to your clients on your behalf.
  • To send email invoices and reminders via our email delivery provider.
  • To process your subscription payment and issue a subscription receipt.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To improve the service via anonymised usage analytics only.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose not listed above.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the following essential service providers, each bound by their own strict privacy terms:

  • Email delivery provider: Receives your clients' email addresses and the invoice content needed to deliver emails on your behalf.
  • Payment processors: Process your subscription payment. We share only your name and email for payment processing; they never receive your invoice or client data.
  • Messaging platform: The underlying platform through which the bot operates. Your messages are subject to the platform's own privacy policies.
  • Website analytics provider: Collects anonymised session data to help us improve the service. Does not receive your invoice or client data.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely in a database on our production servers. Database files are not publicly accessible. Backups are encrypted. PDF invoices are stored temporarily during generation and delivery; they are not retained on our servers permanently.

We use encrypted protocols for all web traffic and bot communications.

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription and request deletion, we will permanently delete your account, client records, and invoice history within 30 days. Payment receipts may be retained for up to 7 years for accounting and legal compliance purposes.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update any incorrect information (most fields are editable directly in the bot).
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export — export your invoice and customer data to CSV at any time from within the bot.

To exercise any of these rights, email hi@invois.work. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our website uses no first-party cookies. Third-party analytics providers may set cookies for tracking purposes. You can opt out of this tracking by using a browser extension that blocks analytics scripts.

9. Children

The bot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at hi@invois.work and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify active subscribers via the bot before the changes take effect. Continued usage of the bot after such changes are announced constitutes your acceptance of the revised privacy policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hi@invois.work. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
